Why treatment at home works

You do not have to go anywhere. And we see straight away how you move at home.

No travel, no waiting room

No painful drive and no hunting for parking. We come to you.

Exercises in your own home

Your stairs, your chair, your bed: the exercises fit what you really do at home.

One to one

You are the only patient during the visit, and you always see the same therapist.

Someone close can join

A partner or carer can listen in and learn how best to help you.

Where does it hurt?

Choose the area and ask your question straight away. Your choice is already filled in on the form.

  • NeckStiff neck, pain after long sitting
  • ShoulderPain when lifting, less movement
  • Elbow and wristOveruse, recovery after a fracture
  • Upper backTension, posture complaints
  • Lower backLow back pain, stiffness
  • HipPain when walking, recovery after surgery
  • KneePain on stairs, recovery after surgery
  • Ankle and footSprains, overuse

This list is not a diagnosis. After an accident, or with severe or sudden symptoms, contact your doctor or call 112.

Treating the whole person, not just the symptom

We look at the root causes of your complaint: your work and home stress, overall body condition, nutrition, posture and the patterns held in your muscles. That is how we help you recover quickly and keep the result.
