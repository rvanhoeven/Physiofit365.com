Privacy policy

Who is responsible

PhysioFit365, Mellany Ginny van Hoeven, Calle Agata Buzón 77, 03170 Rojales (Alicante), Spain. Email: info@physiofit365.com.

What we collect

Only what you fill in on the contact form or the appointment form: name, email address, phone number, address, town or area, your preferred date and time, the subject of your question, your message and whether you prefer a reply by phone or email. We also store the language of your browser and of the page, so that we can reply in your language, and the time of your request. When you request an appointment, we keep your details as a contact.

Why we collect it

To answer your question and, if you wish, to arrange a home visit. We rely on your consent, which you give by ticking the box before sending the form.

Where it is stored

Requests are stored in a database hosted in the European Union. Only PhysioFit365 can read them. We do not sell or share your details for marketing. When you submit a form, an email notification goes to PhysioFit365 and a confirmation goes to the address you entered, through an email delivery service (Resend).

How long we keep it

No longer than needed to deal with your request and any follow-up care.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to access, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, by emailing info@physiofit365.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the Spanish data protection authority (AEPD).

Cookies

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies.
